# Soldier model references for the Himmer 1968 diorama

Mood board of existing 3D soldiers, collected 8 Nov 2026. Nothing was downloaded except preview thumbnails. No sign-ins, no accounts. 25 picks in 6 groups. Every pick has a thumbnail in `thumbs/`; `index.html` is the same set as a contact sheet.

## Strongest looks, and what can actually be used

1. **Toy-style Vietnam infantryman (pick 01, giobiancoFB).** Soft toy proportions, M1 helmet, worried human face. The closest match to "cartoonish but higher quality" and to the clay direction. **Cannot be used**: no licence, not downloadable. Pure look target.
2. **indie-pixel Military Characters Pack (pick 06, $4.99).** Vietnam-era influenced, rounded helmets, olive utilities, painted PBR textures. Closest to refined low-poly with a soft toon feel. **Maybe usable**: paid royalty-free; confirm the export has glTF or FBX. It has no NVA, no radio operator.
3. **Valiant Hearts style cel shading (pick 18).** Flat colour, dark ink outline, a tired dignified face. The best cel-shaded war reference found, and the clearest answer to "serious but cartoon". **Look only**: student work, not downloadable.
4. **Hand-painted khaki rifleman (pick 12, GrunoKromer).** Painterly low-poly at about 9k tris with a 1K texture; warm khaki and soft folds. The model for the NVA khaki-and-side-cap look. **Look only.**
5. **Figures on a diorama base (picks 23 and 24, plus 04).** Cube-of-ground staging and rounded toy-like units show how to get "toy box" without being silly. **Look only.**

**Usable now (licence allows it, glTF or glTF-convertible, sensible tris for about 150 figures):**

| Pick | Licence | Tris | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 13 Zack WW2 Allied Soldier | CC-BY | 11.7k | M1 helmet and webbing on a soft body; re-skin for Vietnam. 150 x 11.7k = 1.75M tris, fine with instancing |
| 17 Low Poly Soldier -Free | CC-BY | 1.4k | Rigged, 5 animations; modern kit, but proves the pipeline |
| 07 Chaz_B Vietnam Soldier Pack | CC-BY-NC | about 3k each | Six Vietnam US archetypes; memorial use is non-commercial, but ask the author |
| 11 Khaki Soldier with Pith Helmet | CC-BY (AI mesh) | 14k | NVA proxy; quality uneven |
| 21 Kenney Blocky Characters | CC0 | very low | Safe crowd filler; it is the look you want to beat |
| 22 Quaternius Character Soldier | CC0 | 7.9k | Rigged glTF stand-in; chibi-leaning heads |

Notes on licences and files:
- Every "free" Sketchfab pick needs a Sketchfab login to download (glTF is offered there). I did not sign in, so file formats and rigs are as listed on the page, not tested.
- Heavy sculpts (picks 02, 08) would need decimation by 20 to 50 times before they suit 150 figures.
- "None shown" means the listing has no licence and no download. Treat it as all rights reserved. Look only.
- Picks 10 and 11 are AI-generated (Meshy). Picks 19 and 20 are Synty: the licence bars redistributing raw assets, which is awkward for shipping models in a web page.
- Gap: there is no stylised NVA or Viet Cong character in a good quality, downloadable form. Picks 10, 11 and 12 are the nearest. Plan to build NVA from a US base with a pith helmet, khaki tunic, AK or SKS.
- Gap: no cel-shaded soldier with an ink outline exists as a downloadable asset that I found. Plan to build it as a shader on any clean low-poly mesh.
- Not reachable from this machine: itch.io (connection refused), ArtStation, Quaternius.com, CGTrader free-model pages (bot wall), TurboSquid (403), Fab only partly. Pick 14 relies on a search snippet for price.

Direction key: clay = soft toy or sculpted clay look; cel-shaded = flat colour and ink outline; refined low-poly = clean faceted or hand-painted low-poly.
