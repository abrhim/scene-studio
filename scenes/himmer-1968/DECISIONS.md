# Capt Lawrence Himmer, 16 April 1968: decisions so far

**DESIGN.md draft 1 approved by Abram on 2026-11-08 (evening).** The gates passed so far: scope, interview, research, design. Next gates: characters approved on the sheet, then the base, builders, integration, review, delivery.

Abram's answers from the interview on 2026-11-08. These go into DESIGN.md section 10 when it is written.

## The scene

- **The moment:** the mission of 16 April 1968, from Company A's 7 AM patrol to the withdrawal to Hill 689. It should capture the events as they happened.
- **Length:** no 3-minute limit. The scene is in **chapters, and each chapter holds** (loops in place) until the viewer taps Next. The story panel jumps between chapters.
- **Audience:** adults and teens. It is a family-history piece more than a TV loop for small children.
- **Screen:** mostly a computer (Mac or laptop browser). The Raspberry Pi budget does not apply, so a full company can be on the hill.

## The fighting

- **Visible fire:** muzzle flashes and tracers that show roughly who is shooting where. Visual effects are wanted.
- **Sound:** not needed now. Build it visual first and leave room for sound later.
- **Death:** shown plainly and gently. Men fall. **No blood.** Larry's death is shown.

## Command and radio

- Show the leaders making decisions.
- Show each radio message as something visible that travels from radio to radio. That includes the delays (Cahill's 1:20 PM message reached Col Meyers at 3:43 PM) and the Army scouts' sighting that never reached the Marines.

## Words on screen

- **Record only:** quote the words where the sources keep them, and use plain paraphrase marked as paraphrase where only the gist survives. Nothing invented.
- **Note for later:** Abram may want to come back and add reconstructed dialogue, with every invented line marked as reconstructed. Keep the word data in a shape that allows a second, marked layer.

## People

- **Named where sourced:** men the sources name and place (for example 1stLt Hayden on Company A's ridge, PFC David Dee Brown Jr on the southern slope) can be tapped by name, with their source. Everyone else is unnamed.
