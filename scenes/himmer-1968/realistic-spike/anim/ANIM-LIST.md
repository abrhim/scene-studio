# Mixamo clips for the Himmer 1968 figures

Downloaded 2026-11-08 from mixamo.com on Abram's Adobe account, by Claude driving his Chrome. Settings for every file: **FBX Binary, 30 fps, keyframe reduction none, Without Skin**, except `Rifle Walk.fbx`, which is **With Skin** (X Bot mesh and skeleton) and serves as the skeleton reference. Character: Mixamo X Bot. "In Place" was ticked only on `Rifle Crouch Walk` and `Walk With Rifle`; every other locomotion clip carries root motion, which the loader should strip or use. `Prone Forward` was downloaded **mirrored** by mistake (the Mirror box was ticked); it is a usable crawl either way.

Licence: Mixamo's terms allow the animations to be used in personal and commercial projects without attribution; they may not be redistributed as stand-alone animation files. Keep this folder out of any public distribution other than as part of the built scene, or gitignore it if in doubt (decide before the first commit).
