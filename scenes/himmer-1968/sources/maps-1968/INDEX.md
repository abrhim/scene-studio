# 1968 maps and imagery, Khe Sanh area (1/9 Marines, April 1968)

Compiled 2026-11-08. Scratch: /private/tmp/claude-501/himmer-maps-scratch/. No sign-ins, no accounts, no CAPTCHA or bot-wall bypass.

## Headline

The best "as of 1968" map is **L7014 sheet 6342-III Huong Hoa, Edition 5-AMS, "Map information as of 1968", "Prepared by AMS, 1968"**, from Texas Tech. The copy we hold is a later 4th printing (June 1973) of that edition. It is the Edition 5 the Marines carried, and its Hill 689 and Hill 552 are on it. Hill 689, Hill 552, the Rock Quarry area, Route 9, Khe Sanh village and Lang Vei are all on this one sheet, so no adjacent sheet is needed for the battle area. The base is near the east edge, E 84.1.

Our existing UT copy (sources/map-6342-III-huong-hoa-AMS-L7014.pdf) is Edition 6 USARPAC, "map information as of 1971". Keep it for comparison only.

  - compare-6342-III-Ed6-info1971-UT-wide-E77-86-N37-43.5-150dpi.png (same window from our 1971 PDF)
- Crop calibration is by label positions, good to about 0.1 km. The scan is paler than UT's and the colour is not comparable.
- Differences from the 1971-information edition (visual comparison of the wide crops, not a full audit):
  - 1971 adds: "Lang Chanh Heliport (Abandoned)" and "Khe Sanh Heliport, Elev 440m" labels; Route 608 shields; extra index-contour and spot-height numbers (600, 500, 400); a "Lang Vei (1)" label near the bottom edge.
  - 1968 has none of those. It shows the same road net, Route 9, Khe Sanh (destroyed), Trung Thuan (destroyed), Lang Ta Tuc and Dong Dang "689".
  - Hill 689 triangle labelled "689" in both. The "552" spot and contours appear in both; I did not verify the 552 label at full resolution.
  - Vegetation, trail and stream differences were not systematically checked. At crop scale the stream and trail pattern looks the same; the clearings (white stipple) look the same in both. Treat any finer difference as unverified.

## 2. 1968 operational maps

- **1/9 Command Chronology, April 1968** (SAVED as usmc-1-9-command-chronology-april-1968-TTU-1201056016.pdf, 62 pages, 3.9 MB). Source: https://vva.vietnam.ttu.edu/repositories/2/digital_objects/224331 , file https://vva.vietnam.ttu.edu/images.php?img=/images/1201/1201056016.pdf . I read pages 1 to 5 and 56, and skimmed the page sizes. It holds Part I to IV: narrative, S-2/S-3 journal, After Action Report Operation Pegasus. **It contains no map or overlay pages** (every page is text, journal or report). Useful facts: on 4 April 1/9 was at Hill 471 (XD 847 354); 1/9 seized Hill 552 (XD 823 403) and Hill 689 (XD 803 409) between 4 and 8 April (the narrative says 1/9 was the first unit to move out of the base, taking Hill 471, then Hill 552 and Hill 689); 1/9 was relieved on 552 and 689 by 3/4 Marines on 21 April, then moved to Operation Kentucky II. The grid XD 803 409 for Hill 689 matches the user's coordinates.
- **Shulimson et al., The Defining Year, 1968** (PDF from archive.org item USMarinesInVietnamTheDefiningYear1968; the usmcu.edu and marines.mil originals return 403 to scripts). The printed page is PDF page minus 16. Saved:
  - usmc-defining-year-p71-allied-enemy-units-khe-sanh-jan1968-on-AMS-base.png (printed p.71). This map is drawn on a reduced AMS 1:50,000 base (grid 77 to 91 visible) and shows Hill 689, 861, 881N/S, 558, 950 and Lang Vei. Closest thing to a "carried" base.
  - usmc-defining-year-p262-marine-allied-units-khe-sanh-feb1968.png (printed p.262). Schematic of the base only.
  - The book's Map Listing has no other Khe Sanh map. Printed pp.312 to 316 hold photographs only, not maps. The Route 9 map on p.121 is the Camp Carroll area, not Khe Sanh, so I did not keep it.
- **The Battle for Khe Sanh (Shore, 1969)** (archive.org TheBattleForKheSanh): only schematic sketch maps (Map 2 Northern Quang Tri, Map 3 Khe Sanh Valley with Hill 861, 881, 558, 950, Map 7 Khe Sanh Combat Base). No topographic detail. Not saved.
- "Khe Sanh: Ringed by Fire": not retrieved.

## 3. Aerial and satellite imagery

USGS EarthExplorer (no login) search at 16 38' 49" N, 106 41' 13" E, datasets Declass 1 and Declass 2 returned 61 scenes (37 CORONA KH-4/4A/4B frames 1966 to 1972, 24 older Declass 2 frames 1962 to 1964). Every CORONA frame is a thin strip about 250 km long by 15 to 25 km wide, so each one contains the point. Public previews only: the `thumbnail` image is 65 x 5 px and the `browse` image is 1480 x 115 px (about 180 m per pixel), so neither shows hills. Full resolution needs login and a paid scan. I did not get it.

I checked footprints from the corner coordinates in each thumbnail's JPEG comment. All listed frames below contain the point. Saved browse strips: corona-previews/ (19 files).

Frames in or near the battle window (entity ID, date, camera):
- DS1102-2103DA058, DA059, DF052: 1967-12-16, Stereo High (best pre-siege resolution)
- DS1045-1069DF078: 1968-01-29; DS1045-2148DA083 / DF085: 1968-02-03; DS1045-2211DA108 / DF111: 1968-02-07 (Stereo Medium, siege period)
- **DS1046-1101DA168 and DS1046-1101DF168: 1968-03-21, Stereo Medium, the last frames before 1/9 took the hills.** The strip crop at the point is cloudy.
- After: DS1049-2119DA237 (1968-12-20), DS1105-2280DF065 (1968-11-21, High), DS1106-2119DF149 (1969-02-13, High), DS1050-1006DA128 / DF128 (1969-03-20).
- Earlier: DS1101-1006DA128 / DF122 (1967-09-16), DS1101-2165DA065 (1967-09-26), DS1043-2165DA044 (1967-08-18), DS1040-2133DA140 / DF138 (1967-04-08), DS1031-1022DA077 / DF075 (1966-04-09), DS1030-1006DF156 (1966-03-10).
- Declass 2 (1962 to 1964) frames are far off in latitude and not useful.
- Ground-level 1968 aerial photos of Hill 689, 552 and the western hills: not found in public sources I could reach. The Defining Year has oblique photos of Khe Sanh (Abel, Duncan collections) but I did not extract them.
